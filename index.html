01 Selected work

Systems and research I designed or led, from benchmark to open-source release.

FairLens

First author · 2026

LLM evaluation framework

Measures vision–language model behaviour on high-stakes questions across hiring, legal, and healthcare settings. I designed the prompt suite — 69 closed- and open-ended prompts per image — and proposed three complementary metrics: soundness against logical ground truth, demographic parity, and unsupported-association rate. The automated harness combines rule-based scoring with LLM-as-a-judge grading.

  • 8 models evaluated
  • 103K+ image–question pairs per model

GPT-5.2-reasoning · Qwen2.5-VL · Qwen3-VL · InternVL3 · Ovis2.5 · LLaVA-1.6 · Llama-3.2-Vision · CogVLM

HumaniBench

ACM TIST 2026

Human-centric multimodal benchmark

A benchmark suite evaluating multimodal LLMs across seven human-centred principles — fairness, ethics, reasoning, robustness, and language inclusivity among them. The core challenge was defining evaluation criteria in domains where no ground truth exists.

  • 15 multimodal LLMs
  • 32K+ human-aligned pairs

GenerativeSSL

AAAI 2025

Self-learning framework

Combines diffusion-based generative augmentation with self-supervised learning so models improve from their own generated views — no additional labels required.

Applies prompt tuning and optimization to the Segment Anything Model: learned soft prompts replace manual prompting, making segmentation of complex remote-sensing imagery scalable.

Bias in the Picture

NeurIPS 2025 WS

Social-cue bias benchmark

A news-image benchmark testing how vision–language models shift their answers when visual social cues — age, gender, race, occupation — are present, graded by an LLM-as-a-judge pipeline with human verification.

  • 1,343 annotated image–question pairs
